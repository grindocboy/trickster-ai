Texas Hold'em Basics

Make the best five-card poker hand using any combination of your two hole cards and the five community cards.

  1. Preflop: receive two cards and act after the blinds.
  2. Flop: three community cards.
  3. Turn: one more community card.
  4. River: the final community card.
  5. Showdown: the best five-card hand wins the pot.

This trainer uses simplified no-limit betting. A raise amount means your total contribution for the current street.
